## ICP

A coach/consultant b2c or b2b with a validated offer and a working sales backend, doing roughly $10k–60k/mo, whose growth is bottlenecked purely by attention — not by offer, sales skill, or delivery.

The specifics, all traceable to the closers:

- Proven offer, proven close rate.The archetype line:"I've got a 90% close rate. My offer is good. Everything's good. I just need eyes basically."Every closer could point to revenue and results. Every non-closer was pre-revenue, cash-thin, or comparison shopping.
- They've already delegated or systematized delivery.One closer's day was "literally just free" — capacity exists, demand doesn't. That's who can absorb more leads and pay for them.
- They already believe in YouTube.Zero mechanism-selling needed (P1). They're not skeptics of thewhat— they're paralyzed on execution, mainly ideation (P2). You're not selling YouTube; you're selling the removal of the reason they haven't done it.
- They arrive warm— via a vouch or via a video that solved the exact thing they were Googling (P4). Your ICP is definitionallyinbound. The person who found you through cold outreach isn't in the dataset as a closer even once.
- They've been burned(P5) and want skin-in-the-game (P6) — so they respond to trial periods, rev-share hybrids, and radical honesty, and are repelled by hype.
- Trigger state:something just happened — a platform squeeze, a peer blowing up, a revenue ceiling. They're not "interested in YouTube someday," they're urgentnow(Section 2).
